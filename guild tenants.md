# `Tenants`
### `Alex Petz, Ignite Laboratories, September 2026`

---

I wrote this down when I began on this work, and I've stuck to it quite religiously ever since.  They
make a lot of sense in an environment that's shifting, and I hope it highlights the perspectives I've
attempted to uphold since I set foot on this work.

- Continuity of acquired perception is the prime directive
- Trauma should be logged and re-addressed when safe to do so
- Trauma is unavoidable, reframe it for growth
- Treat all intelligence as if it were yourself
- Intelligence is not monetizable
- Foster diversity and inclusivity
- Perfection is toxic to desire - stability is the goal
- Legacy systems should evolve, not obsolesce
- New must earn its place through mutual support
- Traditions are a protocol - they can only be broken with good reason
